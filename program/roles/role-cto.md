# Chief Technology Officer

The executive accountable for the security program as a whole. Security
decisions that change what the company sells, what it spends, or what risk it
carries end here.

## Responsibilities

- Approve `pol-information-security` and the annual security plan.
- Own the residual risk the company accepts, as recorded in the risk register.
- Chair the security committee and report to the board twice a year.
- Sponsor the ISO 27001 certification and the annual SOC 2 audit.

## Authority

- Accepts risks rated `high` and `critical`; anything lower is delegated to the CISO.
- Approves exceptions to standards that last longer than six months.
