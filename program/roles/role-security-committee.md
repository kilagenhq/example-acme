# Security Committee

Chaired by the CTO; the CISO, the IT Manager and the heads of engineering,
finance and legal sit on it. Meets quarterly, plus on demand for an incident
rated `critical`.

## Responsibilities

- Approve policies and standards, and record the approval in the document's
  revision history.
- Review the risk register and the capability roadmap once a quarter.
- Decide on exceptions escalated by the CTO.
- Receive the results of the annual penetration test and the DR exercise.

## Authority

- The approval body for every `pol-*` and `std-*` in this repository.
- Can direct a capability investment outside the annual planning cycle.
